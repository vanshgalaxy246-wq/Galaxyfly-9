UK Flights to Grenada: Airport, Routes and Cabin Tips

For UK travellers · No invented prices · Galaxy Fly

Grenada is often called the Spice Island. For travellers leaving the UK, the journey is a long-haul flight into the southern Caribbean. This guide covers the essentials: the arrival airport, how routings often work, and when Business Class is worth considering.

Maurice Bishop International Airport (GND)

Most international flights to Grenada arrive at Maurice Bishop International Airport, code GND. When you compare tickets from London, Manchester or other UK cities, GND is the destination code you should expect to see.

Some itineraries include a connection in another Caribbean or North American city before GND. That is common. What matters is total travel time, connection quality and whether your bags are checked through.

Business Class from the UK to Grenada

Business Class on this route is mainly about the long sector. If your ticket includes a short regional leg, premium benefits may apply only on part of the journey. Ask for a clear breakdown when you request a quote.

UK departure cities

London remains the most common starting point. Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Glasgow also appear in customer enquiries, sometimes via a connecting flight. Flexibility on departure airport can improve cabin availability.
