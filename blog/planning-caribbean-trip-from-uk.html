Planning a Caribbean Trip from the UK: Flights First

For UK travellers · No invented prices · Galaxy Fly

Caribbean holidays from the UK look simple in a brochure and more complex when you book flights. Different islands use different airport codes. Schedules change by season. Cabin inventory comes and goes. Starting with a clear flight plan saves stress later.

Pick the island and the airport code

Grenada is GND, Barbados is BGI, Antigua is ANU, Jamaica often uses MBJ or KIN. Matching your accommodation region to the right airport avoids long transfers after a long-haul flight.

Fix a date window, not only one day

If you can travel a day earlier or later, you often see more cabin options — including Business Class. Rigid single-date searches are the least flexible way to buy long-haul tickets.

Decide cabin priority early

If Business Class is important, say so at the start of your enquiry. If economy is fine, we can focus on schedule and total journey time instead.

Galaxy Fly helps UK travellers structure that process: destination, dates, departure city, cabin, then a real quote.

Start with a destination — for example Grenada — or contact us with your plans.
